Keep it respectful.

A few simple guidelines help everyone enjoy the community.

01 · Respect everyoneBe polite. Harassment, hate, and targeted abuse aren't welcome.
02 · Use the right channelsKeep messages relevant and follow each channel's purpose.
03 · Protect your privacyNever share passwords, account tokens, authentication codes, or private information.
04 · No suspicious filesDon't run unknown downloads or commands sent by people you don't trust.
05 · Listen to staffFollow server rules and contact staff if you need help.
06 · Keep things constructiveDisagreements happen; keep discussions calm and respectful.

Quick questions.

Some helpful answers for new members.

How do I join Client Hub?

Use one of the Join Discord buttons on this page. Remember to add your real server invite link to the website first.

How do I open a support ticket?

Join the Discord server and look for the ticket channel or ticket panel. Follow the instructions posted there.

Where can I find the rules?

Check the rules channel in the Discord server. The guidelines on this website are a quick overview, not a replacement for the server's official rules.

Can staff help with a problem?

Contact the appropriate staff member through the server's approved support channels. Never send anyone your password or login token.
